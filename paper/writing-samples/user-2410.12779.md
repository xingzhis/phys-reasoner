## Patterns to adopt for this paper

- Sentence rhythm: short declarative topic sentences followed by longer qualifying sentences. Avoid compound subjects that stack clauses.
- Acronym discipline: introduce at first use in abstract, then use everywhere. Our candidates: TIR, CoT, GRPO (all established); new acronyms avoid.

## Patterns not to adopt
